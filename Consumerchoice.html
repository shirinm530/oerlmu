Utility Maximization: the Best Affordable Bundle

A consumer splits a budget between goods X and Y. The optimum sits where the budget line is tangent to the highest reachable indifference curve — where MRS = px/py. Change prices, income, and tastes.

Try it: Move the sliders to change prices, income, or the preference weight — or jump straight into a scenario with the preset buttons (Cheap X, High Income, Prefers Y). Watch the optimum slide along the budget line, and check the tangency box to confirm MRS still equals px/py at the new bundle.
