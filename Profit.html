PROFIT MAXIMIZATION AND SUPPLY

Cost Curves & the Firm's Supply Decision

From a firm's cost function come four curves: MC, ATC, AVC, and AFC. Marginal cost cuts through the minimum of both average curves. Set the price to see where the firm produces — and when it shuts down.

Try it: Slide the price down until it crosses the AVC minimum — the firm shuts down and q* drops to zero. Raise it to the ATC minimum for the break-even point, and further still for a profit. Use the presets to jump straight to each scenario, or switch to Firm Supply to watch these same points trace out the supply curve.
