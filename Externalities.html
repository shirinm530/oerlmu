Private vs. Social Optimum

When a transaction affects third parties, the price system misses part of the true cost or benefit. Compare the market outcome to the socially optimal outcome, then test how a policy (tax, subsidy, or quota) can close the gap.

Try it: Pick a scenario below — say, Negative Production — and watch the SMC curve rise above PMC, opening up the red deadweight-loss triangle. Then scroll to Policy Response, choose the Pigovian Tax, and drag the strength slider until Market Q lines up with Social Q*. Notice the red triangle shrink to nothing as the policy fully internalizes the externality.
Choose a different scenario: Each externality type calls for a different fix. Try Positive Consumption (e.g., vaccinations or education) — the market under-consumes, so only a Subsidy can pull it up toward Q*. Try Negative Production or Negative Consumption and compare a Tax against a blunt Quota — both can eliminate the deadweight loss, but they work through different mechanisms.
