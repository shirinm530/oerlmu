Two people, two goods, one fixed pie. Whenever Gilligan's and Mary Ann's marginal rates of substitution differ, a mutually beneficial trade exists. Compare the initial endowment to the Pareto-efficient contract curve, then switch to Competitive Prices to see how a single price ratio can guide both consumers to an efficient allocation on their own.

Try it: Drag Gilligan Initial Bananas down to 20 and watch the gray Endowment dot slide left — a green‑shaded Lens opens between Gilligan's and Mary Ann's indifference curves, meaning trade is mutually beneficial. Toggle Show Contract Curve to see the purple curve, then click Competitive Prices and drag Relative Price (Pₓ/Pᵥ) until the blue Price Line becomes tangent to both curves at once. Watch the blue Competitive Equilibrium dot snap onto the purple contract curve, MRSG and MRSM converge to the same value, the lens vanish, and Pareto Efficient? flip to Yes.
